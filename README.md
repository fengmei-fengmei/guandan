We have two pages: one for 4-player Guandan card counting and the other for 6-player Guandan card counting.

Operation instruction:
For example, if your partner plays a single card, tap the single-card counter for your partner, and the count will increase to 1. When your partner has no single cards left, tap the "None" button, and the count will reset to 0.
